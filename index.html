LOOKING AFTER YOUR GLASSES

A few habits that make lenses and frames last

Skip the dry wipeRinse lenses under water first — wiping them dry drags dust across the surface and causes fine scratches.
Use a microfibre clothTissue, clothing, and paper towels are all slightly abrasive. A clean microfibre cloth is the safe option.
Store them closed, lenses upA closed case protects against knocks. Resting frames face-down scratches the lenses on any hard surface.
Keep them out of the heatA hot car dashboard or direct sun can warp frames and damage some lens coatings.
Use both hands to remove themTaking glasses off one-handed slowly stretches and misaligns the frame over time.
Bring them in if the fit feels offLoose or uneven frames are usually a quick adjustment — better to fix early than force them.
